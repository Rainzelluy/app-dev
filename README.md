# My Favorite Animated Series 🦎🦉
This repository contains information about my two all-time favorite shows. 

## 1. Amphibia
**Amphibia** is an American animated fantasy comedy television series created by Matt Braly. 

### Why I love it:
* **The World-Building:** The swampy, frog-inhabited world of Amphibia is incredibly detailed and fun to explore.
* **Character Growth:** Watching Anne Boonchuy grow from a self-centered teenager into a responsible hero is very satisfying.
* **The Humor:** It balances hilarious slice-of-life moments with epic, emotional story arcs.

> *"Change can be difficult, but it's necessary for growth."* 

## 2. The Owl House
**The Owl House** is an American animated fantasy comedy series created by Dana Terrace.

### Why I love it:
1. **Unique Magic System:** The concept of magic being drawn from a bile sac and cast through glyphs is highly creative.
2. **Representation:** It features wonderful LGBTQ+ representation that feels natural and integral to the story.
3. **The Found Family Trope:** The relationship between Luz, Eda, and King is the heart of the show.
